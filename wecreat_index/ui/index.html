About LUOM What's New

      

        LUOM What's New is a free, independent tool made by and for members of the
        Lumos Ultra Owners & Makers (LUOM) Facebook group.
        It is an indexer, searcher and cataloger: it reads WeCreat's public knowledge base at
        help.wecreat.com, lists the articles that
        concern the Lumos Ultra, notes what is new or changed, and links you to each original article.
      

      
What it is not

      
    
        
  • LUOM does not write, maintain, host or own WeCreat's knowledge base. We cannot correct, update or remove any article.
    • 
        
  • This tool is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WeCreat, and it is not an official WeCreat product or support channel.
    • 
        
  • For official help, warranty or support questions, contact WeCreat through help.wecreat.com.
    •

  • The index is kept on your own computer for your personal use. Please do not republish articles or share the index files.
    • 
        
  • WeCreat, Lumos Ultra and MakeIt are names and trademarks of their owner. They are used here only to describe what this tool works with.
